# ADR-0036: A sky for the openings and for rays that leave the scene

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Amends:** ADR-0028 (step 9.6 is added, and the later steps move up by one)

## Context

The legacy factory's biggest room is open to the sky: the level names a sky (`skyName="sky1"`) whose six faces the legacy engine drew behind everything. The new engine showed the fog color there. And from Phase 9 on, rays leave the scene: the path tracer lit them with a flat background color, and diffuse GI (9.9) and reflections (9.10) need to know what they find there. One sky answers both, so it comes before TAA.

## Decision

- **Cube textures in the RHI.** `TextureDesc::cube` makes six square faces, +X, −X, +Y, −Y, +Z, −Z, each with its mips; shaders find them in their own bindless array, `g_cubeTextures` (binding 5, 64 slots). `renderer::CreateCubeTexture` checks that the faces agree, and `AssetLibrary::LoadCubeTexture` loads and caches six image files.
- **The legacy faces are used as they are.** Their names map to the cube's faces in the order right, left, up, down, front, back. The legacy engine was left-handed, so its front is this engine's −Z: one shared lookup, `SkyRadiance` in `MeshTypes.slangh`, flips z.
- **`Atmosphere` holds the sky** and an intensity that multiplies its colors, which are linear light. Without a sky, nothing changes.
- **Drawn where nothing is.** The mesh pass ends with a fullscreen triangle at the far plane (reverse Z: depth 0), tested against the pre-pass's depth and not written: the sky costs only the pixels it covers, and no pass of its own.
- **The path tracer's escaping rays find the sky**, through the same lookup, so the reference and the raster view agree. Later ray-traced steps use it too.
- **The importer** reads `skyName` and copies the six faces to `assets/Factory/Sky/`, with a `sky;name=sky1` node that the sandbox loads. They are the author's own, under the repository's licence.

## Consequences

- The open room shows the legacy clouds. `sky1` is a dark evening sky, and after tonemapping it is darker on screen than the legacy engine drew it; the intensity can raise it if the level needs it. Its gradients are dark enough that BC7 blocks would show if it were brightened a lot.
- Path traced, the sky lights what it can see. Ten exposure stops brighter, the open room's walls show light from the opening.
- GPU tests check a cube's face orientation, that the sky shows only where nothing is drawn, and the white furnace with a sky instead of a background color. Synchronization validation stays clean in the factory, raster and path traced.
- The sky does not light rasterized surfaces yet: the ambient term still does, until diffuse GI (9.9).

## Alternatives considered

- A sky pass of its own before the meshes: it shades every pixel, then the meshes overwrite most of them.
- An equirectangular texture: the legacy sky is six faces, and a cube samples without a pole seam.
- Rotating the faces when importing instead of flipping the lookup: rewrites the author's images for a convention the shader expresses in one line.
